# Module 03 — Email Management  

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Configure and troubleshoot mail delivery  
- Implement spam protection and authentication  

## 🗂️ Key Topics  
- Exim basics (mail transport agent)  
- SpamAssassin, cPanel Email Filters  
- Authentication: DKIM, SPF  
- Forwarders, autoresponders, mail routing  

## 🛠️ Practice  
- Enable DKIM/SPF for a domain  
- Test mail delivery with an external email provider  
- Configure autoresponder and email filters
