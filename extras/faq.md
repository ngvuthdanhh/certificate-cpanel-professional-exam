# FAQ — cPanel Professional Exam

**Q: Do I need root access knowledge?**  
A: Yes, basic WHM & CLI operations are tested.

**Q: Is the exam open book?**  
A: No, closed-book, but practical knowledge is emphasized.

**Q: What’s the passing score?**  
A: Typically ~80% (official cPanel doesn’t always disclose exact score).

**Q: How long is the certification valid?**  
A: 2 years.
